- The chantier pipeline (1 done, 2 sent, 3 order received/to receive, 4 received, 5 invoiced, 6 deposited/paid) comes only from browser-safe `src/lib/etape-chantier.ts`, read by `getSuiviChantiers` (one row per chantier share of an attachment) and shown on `/suivi` + dashboard; reception is offered only once an order exists, even for already-invoiced chantiers, so each chantier sits at exactly one step and nothing changes billing state. `bilan-reception.functions.ts` remains only for the emailed report.
